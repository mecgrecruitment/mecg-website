## Recommendation

Make **Operate** a mode within the existing board event/round destinations, not another dashboard destination. A board operator needs to answer “What needs intervention now?” A member needs “Who or which team am I observing, can I edit, and did that save?” Give those roles different compositions over the same authorized contracts.

Preserve the bold blue/orange identity in a compact private workspace: blue selected navigation and primary actions, orange ink for attention, white surfaces on cool paper, 4px rhythm, 8px controls, 12px panels, 44px touch targets and the existing Avenir/Segoe/Helvetica/Arial system sans stack. Keep names and rubric descriptions readable; reserve mono for identifiers, times and figures. Use shared `src/app/workspace.css` roles rather than copying historical local styles. No marketing hero, new font, novelty timer animation, decorative metric cards or new scoring system.

**Hypothesis:** reducing setup controls during a live event and giving recovery a fixed location will reduce search and mistaken action. Validate with realistic event scenarios; make no numerical performance promise.

## Non-negotiable state and policy boundaries

1. Attendance, RSVP, round enrollment, group placement, staffing, saved evidence, qualifying evidence, locked submission, decision release and notification delivery are separate facts. A reservation is not arrival; arrival is not merit; two assigned people are not two valid independent evaluations.
2. Keep current policy: Speed Dating weight 40, Group Case weight 60, Hash no numeric contribution; 1–5/N/O semantics, required observations, rubric minimum, at least 75% observed weight, two valid independent evaluations, and a spread of at least 25 points entering review. Never compensate for a missed rotation or late arrival by inventing scores or reducing quorum. General observations never enter scores/readiness/rank.
3. Preserve conflict enforcement, cycle permissions, independent reads and blind screening. Names/headshots already permitted in live grading do not authorize a named screening queue. No evaluator sees another evaluator's ratings or notes through the proposed member view. Board-only prior-round context must stay board-only.
4. **Observed policy mismatch requiring P0 resolution:** `20261016191000_grading_contract_consistency.sql` lets `rate_speed_dating_applicant` update an evaluation stored as `SUBMITTED` while grading remains active. Group Case's dedicated roster also treats inline persistence as editable evidence (`member/page.tsx` explicitly explains this distinction; its revision migration is `20261016202000_group_case_write_revisions.sql`). The generic form and product policy describe submitted evaluations as immutable. Do not broaden these mutations or represent a saved inline row as a newly immutable submission. Engineering/Board must decide the explicit draft-evidence versus locked-submission contract, migration/backfill and correction path before unifying completion language. Preserve locked records and historical evidence; do not silently relabel the database as a policy fix.
5. Close grading remains consequential: `round-status-control.tsx` loads canonical coverage, explains no transition back to open, and reconciles unknown write outcomes. Moving this control must preserve all of that. Closing check-in/event and freezing the evaluation round remain separate actions. Frozen work is not released; released decisions are not delivered email.

## Board control room contract

**Primary task:** identify the current operational blocker and resolve it through an existing authorized action. **Primary result:** a server-confirmed attendance, placement, assignment or status receipt with a refreshed source view. **Supporting evidence:** event/cycle, check-in window, grading availability, last successful read and the distinct staffing/evidence states.

Keep `/admin/events/[id]` as the attendance/room entry. Add a compact view selector: **Operate · Schedule · Settings**. Keep `/admin/rounds/[roundId]` as the round evidence/staffing authority, with **Operate · Setup · Results** where permitted. These are proposed local sections/query views, not new top-level products. A useful first increment needs no saved mode preference or automatic live-mode switch.

In Operate, show this order:

1. A plain event title, cycle, location and server-derived window/status. A compact line distinguishes “Check-in open” from “Grading active” only when the round is explicitly known. Put “Refresh” and “Last successful refresh …” beside it; do not advance that timestamp after a failed read.
2. A prominent **Record assisted check-in** action and **Show check-in QR** disclosure. Keep exact identifier/reason entry together; hide cancellation, time edits, rubric changes and QR rotation inside their relevant settings/context. QR rotation remains visibly destructive to the old link.
3. A short **Needs action** queue. First increment: attendance load failed, slot list unavailable, unplaced Group Case applicants, under-staffed applicants, and unknown/incomplete evidence, each within its existing permission scope. Unknown is a distinct state and does not inflate a zero count. Each queue entry explains the fact and links to the exact existing event, group/assignment controls or results section. Do not infer “late,” “absent evaluator” or “next rotation” from current data.
4. The searchable attendance roll or coverage list, labeled precisely. Prefer ordinary rows over separate statistic cards. Put observation text behind an authorized disclosure, not next to the shared QR. Retain the original eight-second cadence initially; add visibility/focus-aware refresh only after verifying it will not reset open forms or overwrite local edits.
5. A secondary **Review round** link and relevant settings links. Keep detailed scores out of the default room-control surface; this changes default presentation, not permissions.

**Dependencies:** the initial mode reorders existing data/actions. A combined event/round control room needs an explicit link: the inspected `events` shape has cycle/kind/times but no `round_id` (`src/lib/supabase/database.types.ts`, `events`; event creation has no round picker). Same cycle/name/kind is not a safe join. Until a link exists, offer separate authorized round navigation and do not claim the event controls a particular round. Do not join privileged result data into an operations endpoint to fill a missing badge.

**Acceptance:** on a narrow phone, an operator can record attendance without traversing settings; a failed attendance fetch cannot show “0 arrived”; a walk-up with no application is counted and explicitly distinguishable from applicant coverage; a committee/operator account cannot gain aggregate or protected evidence access; a score spread is not presented as missing staffing; switching views preserves pending entry or warns before leaving.

## Feature workflows and recommended changes

### 1. Event setup, publication and scheduling — P1

**Existing:** `/admin/events/new` captures name/kind/visibility/location, Detroit-time start/end and check-in window; successful creation returns a raw token once. Event settings edit those values and cancel with a reason; slots are individually created through `CREATE_SLOT`. The round and event overviews already group scheduling with interviews. References: `events/new/event-form.tsx`, `[id]/event-controls.tsx`, `[id]/slot-form.tsx`, `/api/admin/events`, `/api/admin/rush`.

**Proposed workflow:** choose event details → review a human-readable Detroit-time summary and visibility → create → show receipt and QR → add slots → inspect exact slots/capacity → operate. Keep event time, admission window and RSVP slots together in Schedule; keep round publication/start/freeze in the round. Rename the current “Date” field to “Event starts.” Display full slot start/end, capacity and confirmed reservation count; current event detail renders only slot start/capacity even though the query loads ends. The existing overview supplies aggregate reservations, not an event-detail per-slot applicant manifest.

**New composition:** a small chronological list of slots with full dates/timezone and an editing summary. Client-side suggestions may prefill check-in boundaries, but must be explicitly editable and never assumed to be policy. Do not default-open admissions by changing times as a side effect of event creation.

**New contract/schema only if approved:** batch slot generation needs validated conflict/capacity rules, duplicate protection and atomic/idempotent create semantics; updating/deleting occupied slots needs explicit existing-booking treatment and notification semantics. No drag-to-reschedule or “apply to all” until those contracts exist. Rooms and member availability are not captured by the inspected event form; overlap warnings from event timestamps alone can only say “time overlaps,” not “this member/room is double-booked.”

**Acceptance:** crossing midnight/DST retains the intended instant through `detroit-date-time.ts`; public/invite/internal labels retain the actual publication boundary; repeat creation after a lost response does not silently duplicate events/slots (new idempotency work if not already supplied); cancellation retains RSVPs in audit and does not claim message delivery. Linking calendar export must retain `/api/events/[id]/ics` authorization/visibility behavior; do not invent calendar synchronization.

### 2. Arrival and identity — P0/P1

**Existing workflow:** scan active token → resolve identity → sign in/create account if needed → return → submit → confirmed/already recorded. Staff fallback: verify in person → exact applicant identifier + reason → RPC checks event window/access/person → recorded/duplicate. Latest walk-up SQL supersedes the earlier application-only assisted lookup: an existing person can be recorded without an application. This does not create an arbitrary new person from a mistyped staff entry.

**New composition:** lead staff mode with one exact identifier field, reason and an explicit outcome. Keep identity input private and clear it after success using current behavior. On duplicates, say “Already recorded” rather than a generic success. Explain that an account without an application can attend when supported; correct “applicant account” copy that implies a submitted application is required. Add a member-appropriate return to My work using an authorized role-aware result; the current POST response has only a duplicate boolean, so role-specific confirmation may need a small response contract rather than guessed routing.

**Identity privacy:** use an applicant-displayed existing code where available or exact uniqname/email; do not require the screening code universally because not every walk-up has one. Do not expose an unauthenticated fuzzy name/email lookup. Preserve generic failed-match errors, exact-match ambiguity rejection, operator audit/reason codes and server permission checks. A visual “confirmed person” preview before recording would need a new restricted lookup/confirmation contract; avoid adding it casually as a search endpoint. Shared screen mode must show only event/QR/instructions and never the attendance list, contact information, notes, scores or query-string token in the surrounding UI.

**QR behavior:** raw token is shown only at creation/rotation in current source. Offer a dedicated QR-only view from that authorized context, with clear “rotating invalidates previous code” copy and a printable local QR option if approved. Do not imply the existing digest can regenerate the old raw token. Moving raw tokens out of URL/history, controlling referrers/cache and masking them in telemetry is a security/transport follow-up, not an already-completed design feature.

**Late arrivals:** within the open window, use the same duplicate-safe check-in; arrival never changes stage or score. After closure, show the real closed state and direct to Board. Staff-assisted SQL also enforces the window; it is not an override. An authorized time-window edit is distinct from an exception and must remain deliberate. Any “record late with reason” exception/backdating needs new approved policy, audit fields and RPC; do not add `LATE` to the existing assistance reason list without that work. No automated merit penalty or guaranteed alternate assessment.

**Connectivity:** an attendee without connectivity can ask a connected operator for the existing assisted route. If both devices cannot reach the backend, neither can promise attendance was recorded. Show “Not confirmed” and retry guidance. A locally typed identifier is not a server receipt. No offline attendance queue or paper-to-system import is established by source.

**Acceptance:** test member, submitted applicant, signed-in walk-up without application, withdrawn applicant, wrong token, expired token, identity lookup failure, duplicate, ambiguous identifier and lost acknowledgement separately. No account is switched implicitly. Read/record permissions remain server-side. The view never treats everyone absent from applicant coverage as unchecked-in.

### 3. Speed Dating: people in front of the member — P0/P1

**Existing:** current interface is one sitting, each member scores applicants actually met; no pre-assignment is required. Migration `20260913180000_speed_dating_roster_rating.sql` describes two-minute rotations past member tables in applicant pairs; earlier session/pair/table migrations describe older workflows. Preserve the actual rotation/scoring policy; do not reactivate older two-session pairing rules because their tables still exist. Current `SESSION = 1` is not a room-wide rotation clock.

**Proposed workflow:** confirm round/editability → find the person in front of you → identify them using already-authorized name/headshot → review anchor → rate → see confirmed save → optionally add note → return to find. Keep two recently selected people in a compact **In front of me** working set as an optional member-local choice. This is not a persisted pair, a claim, an official match or proof they were met; no new scoring relationship is created. Retain full search and an explicit clear selection. A remembered person should never be mistaken for the next official rotation.

**New composition:** an optional “Needs save” filter and last confirmed save time. Separate “Rated on this page” from “Saved” when taps are pending; calculate confirmed counts from acknowledged rows. Show existing 1–5 anchors without forcing another page. Keep the 44px stars; expose each score's semantic label and selection to keyboard/screen readers. Long names and failed headshots must not shift buttons off-screen.

**P0 new contract:** bring Speed Dating writes to the revision/mutation receipt discipline already used by Group Case. Client request sequencing does not prevent an older in-flight server write from finishing after a newer one or protect another tab/device. Define expected revision, stable mutation ID, canonical snapshot, serialized per-person writes, unknown outcome, explicit reconciliation and conflicts. Keep the last intended score/note visible on errors, distinct from confirmed data. The current client rolls the score back while retaining note text; do not count that as durable recovery.

**Notes:** current API requires a numeric score and the UI says “Pick a star to save” for note-only text. Do not encourage an arbitrary score just to preserve a note. P1 options: clearly mark note-only text as unsaved and offer private copy, or add approved note-only evidence persistence through a new contract. Do not advertise cross-device draft/offline support before implementation. Export/private backup contains personal evidence and needs conspicuous handling language.

**Current/next with existing data:** “Current: selected person(s), round active, last save …”; “Next: find the next conversation.” Never “Next: Table 4” without authoritative rotation data. Global roster size is not a member's quota to evaluate everybody they never met. Keep the source statement “grade each applicant you meet,” not “finish the roster.”

**Acceptance:** two rapid taps and two competing tabs cannot silently overwrite newer evidence; a network loss retains exact intended text/score and does not show Saved; leaving during the note debounce exposes unsaved work; a conflict removes protected access rather than merely disabling a star; a frozen/deadline-passed write is refused and the UI explains why; no recommendation changes adjustment, weighting, required criterion or independent-evidence rules.

### 4. Group Case teams, placement and live observation — P1

**Existing setup:** `GroupForms` can form teams from unplaced applicants, create by hand, assign/remove applicants and delete empty teams. `form_case_groups` randomly distributes enrolled unplaced applicants among new teams, balances those new teams and leaves existing teams alone (`20260914150000_form_case_groups.sql`). It is not an optimizer for room/time/attendance/skills. The UI's unplaced estimate uses stage-filtered applications while the RPC uses round enrollment; preview/receipt should reconcile to the canonical eligible cohort.

**Proposed board workflow:** inspect enrolled/unplaced → select team size → explain that existing teams stay unchanged → form → server-confirm created teams/placed people → inspect exceptions → assign evaluators using existing assignment controls → operate. Integrate placement actions into the matching queue rows instead of sending every problem to a generic applicant file. Keep an explicit hand-placement path for late or exceptional arrivals; do not re-form everybody to fit one arrival.

**Placement safety:** show current group and intended destination before an individual change. Never promise drag/drop atomic transfer or one-click undo from separate unassign/assign calls. A single “Move” operation with frozen/live-work checks, expected roster revision, reason and audit is a **new contract**, and a versioned group change log is **new schema** if required. Inspect existing RPC restrictions before choosing when moves are allowed; do not bypass a backend refusal. Preserve already-saved evaluator evidence and history. In a live round, moving people must not silently imply that an evaluator observed their prior work.

**Proposed member workflow:** select actual case team → observe one person → enter only observed dimensions/notes → save acknowledgement → canonical review-quality feedback → continue same team. Keep the existing table preference/search/rubric/recovery features. Rename “Table” to “Case team” unless a physical table is authoritatively mapped: the existing chips are backed by `case_groups`, not a room map. Put selected team and “Observations open / Read-only” above the fold. Offer “Next person in this team” as navigation only, with their identity visible before editing; do not imply official sequence or force scoring.

**Quality:** maintain separate “Started,” “Evidence saved,” “Your review qualifies” and board “Two valid independent reviews.” Count a qualifying review only from `quality.valid`; absent quality stays unavailable. Notes-only evidence must remain savable even when it does not qualify. Preserve leave-unobserved-blank/N/O semantics; never fill missing scores to make progress green. Do not rename a mutable saved observation “Submitted and locked.”

**Late arrival:** after appropriate enrollment/attendance handling, board may use permitted manual placement. Distinguish “Checked in,” “Enrolled,” “Placed,” and “Has qualifying evidence.” A late attendee in a team still needs genuine observation; do not backfill missed behavior. Existing saved selection can be stale after placement changes: validate team existence, show a non-destructive roster-change notice, and let the member reselect while retaining unconfirmed edits. Live roster refresh/assignment polling is **new client/contract work**, not supplied by remembering a chip in localStorage.

**Acceptance:** re-running formation leaves existing teams intact; zero unplaced has a truthful no-change receipt; a missing or stale group is distinct from empty search; team changes cannot discard pending local work; one save cannot mark the whole team complete; ordinary members cannot see prior Speed Dating aggregates or colleagues' evidence; read-only review links remain available after grading closes.

### 5. Coverage, evaluator staffing and results — P0/P1

**Existing:** board Group Case rows already combine assigned evaluators, form statuses and canonical evidence state. `group-case-coverage.ts` deliberately separates counts from validity. `round-board.tsx` derives “graders by group” from evaluators found in results; it does not prove they are physically present or exclusively assigned to that group. `rounds/coverage.ts` is a staffing summary, not evidence qualification. General evaluator load counts open/total assignments, not available time.

**Proposed board workflow:** start with **Needs intervention** → inspect the specific reason → open existing assignment/reassignment/placement/results controls → confirm action → refresh. Use separate filters for “Unplaced,” “Fewer than two assigned,” “Missing valid evidence,” “Spread review,” and “Validity unavailable.” Combine rows only by applicant/team, not by treating those states as equivalent. Operations-only accounts can act on staffing while an authorized decision manager checks evidence.

**High-impact refinement:** place a short reason beside a specific existing action: “One evaluator assigned → Assign evaluator”; “Two assigned, one qualifying review → Review evidence”; “Score spread review → Open results”; “Validity unavailable → Ask an authorized Board reviewer.” Do not direct a third evaluator to replace a low score or claim an automatic rebalancer. Show existing conflict restrictions before choosing a person where the authorized contract allows; never expose conflict reasons to the room.

**Speed Dating:** replace assignment-coverage headlines in the Interviews overview with an authorized grading summary consistent with round detail, or say that coverage is available inside the round. Do not interpret zero pre-assignments as failed staffing. Distinguish raw mean, adjusted result, number of graders and canonical valid independent evaluations. A ranking/results count is not attendance. Keep evaluation content out of a shared live display.

**Acceptance:** a person with two submitted partial observations remains incomplete if the server says so; a recused assignment does not count toward coverage; an inaccessible result stays unknown rather than zero; the same evaluator twice cannot satisfy independence; a staffing adjustment cannot alter submitted evidence. Keep results sorting/export semantics in `speed-dating-results.tsx`, `group-case-results.tsx` and `src/lib/group-case-results.ts` intact.

### 6. General observations and generic evaluations — P0/P1

**General observations:** add name search and explicit event context to `/member/event` as a new composition using its own conflict-filtered roster. Replace “observe X of all checked-in people” completion pressure with “Your saved observations” plus optional browse count; members should record actual encounters. Do not auto-open a person merely because they have no observation if it implies an assignment. Keep neutral tags and factual notes, with no stars or admission recommendation. Save stays explicit unless the endpoint gains a robust autosave contract.

**P0 error handling:** distinguish confirmed closure/access denial from unavailable reads; preserve entered text and selected tags on recoverable failure. The current catch copy already asks the member to retry, but route-level roster errors need typed meaning. Unknown save results need readback/reconciliation before a successful completion claim. A new durable draft/compare-copy contract is required before promising cross-device or outage recovery for this form.

**Generic evaluations:** preserve the existing calibration → draft → review → lock workflow and its browser/server recovery. Show “Next ready assignment” only after acknowledged submission; keep read-only submitted history and conflict reporting. Retain its real requirements and focus recovery. Do not merge these generic immutable submissions with inline save-on-tap semantics until the P0 policy mismatch is resolved.

**Acceptance:** the same outage is not called a closed window; changing events cannot drop unconfirmed observations silently; saved observation does not add score/readiness credit; generic evaluation cannot lock on an unconfirmed draft revision; a submitted form has no direct edit control. Observation navigation and recovery tests must be independent of grading tests because their contracts differ.

### 7. Closing and handoff — P0

**Workflow:** review attendance separately → review canonical round coverage/results → inspect unfinished and spread-review cases → explicit close-grading confirmation → verify round status → read-only evidence handoff. Reuse `round-status-control.tsx`, `round-close-request.ts`, `/api/admin/round-close` and its server summary. Preserve the ability to close with unfinished work if current policy permits; explain the consequence rather than silently changing policy to block it.

Put these status receipts in their proper places: “Attendance recorded”; “Draft/evidence saved”; “Evaluation submitted and locked”; “Grading closed”; “Decision released”; “Notification pending/failed/delivered” only when supported by the relevant downstream record. Do not add a control-room **Finish event** action that performs all of these together. A schedule update or grading freeze must never claim applicants were notified.

**Acceptance:** a lost close response cannot invite a duplicate close; source freshness and no-reopen warning remain visible; read-only evidence remains reachable; no member receives unreleased decisions/aggregates; release and delivery continue through their existing separate authority/workflows.

## Current and next: what can ship honestly

**First increment, existing data:** Board current = selected event/window plus selected round status on its own page; board next = a real queue of existing actionable exceptions. Member current = selected person/case team and editing/save state; member next = another selected person or actual ready task. Label manual selection as manual. Do not infer physical location, queue position or readiness from presence in a roster.

**Later increment, new capability:** an optional event-linked room plan can show a member's published current assignment and next published assignment. It must distinguish **Planned**, **Published**, **Current**, **Changed**, **Completed/closed**, and **Unknown/stale** from evaluation persistence. “Current” comes from an authorized effective plan/version; “next” stays a plan, never a promise. Do not advance a room because someone tapped a score or because one phone's timer elapsed.

Minimal new domain dependencies before that later increment:

- An explicit authorized event↔round relation, with same-cycle validation and a chosen cardinality. Determine whether one event can host multiple rounds and one round spans multiple events before choosing a nullable FK versus join table.
- Event locations/posts with stable identifiers if room/table routing is desired. `case_groups` and legacy Speed Dating `table_number` are not a complete physical room plan.
- Versioned event sessions/rotations with start/end or an explicit operator-controlled active step; planned slots and actual progression must be distinct. Specify operator ownership, clock authority, pause/delay rules, rollback limits and simultaneous-control conflicts. Preserve current physical rotation policy; do not invent a new matching algorithm.
- Published participant/post assignments tied to plan version and role, with effective times, conflict checks, cancelled/replaced assignments and audit. Distinguish applicant pair membership from evaluator placement. Retain historical pair/session data rather than overwriting it.
- Member assignment acknowledgement/presence only if required, as a separate record. A member check-in alone proves neither availability nor acceptance of a staffing post. Acknowledgement is not an evaluation or second independent review.
- A restricted read model returning only the actor's permitted current/next instructions and plan freshness. An operator mutation needs expected revision/idempotency and audit; reconnect needs authoritative readback. Realtime subscriptions are optional delivery machinery, not the domain model or a guarantee of synchronization.
- Any persisted late-arrival exception or correction/import workflow needs explicit policy, actual occurrence versus recording time, actor/reason, duplicate reconciliation, access/retention and immutable history. Current timestamped attendance is insufficient for invented lateness adjudication.

No schema is needed for simple view selection, roster search, ordering existing controls, local working-set selection or revealing full slot times. New schema is also not a prerequisite to fix misleading error/progress copy. Keep these increments separate.

## Phone, privacy and outage requirements

- A single sticky context strip may show selected event/team/person and confirmed-save status. It must not cover focused fields, browser controls or the soft keyboard. Keep controls in document flow when space is tight; avoid a second fixed navigation bar.
- Use 44px targets for live actions and filters, visible text labels for dangerous actions, 16px narrow-screen inputs, wrapped long names and proper focus return from rubric/dialog/disclosure. Do not convey “saved,” “needs attention,” or “selected” through color alone. Respect reduced motion; use only quiet existing state feedback.
- Permission-safe shared mode is a separate projection of event instructions/QR, not CSS hiding private data already fetched. A presenter accidentally backing out must not reveal notes behind the QR. A new standalone shared route must recheck permissions/token exposure rather than accept arbitrary record IDs.
- Separate connection hint from authoritative server state. `navigator.onLine` cannot prove a save succeeded. Show a last successful read, a specific unknown save outcome and the appropriate safe retry/reconcile action. Never show “Live” without a freshness qualifier.
- Keep recovery per feature honest: generic evaluations have draft recovery; Group Case has in-memory unsaved evidence/private export and server revision reconciliation, while its localStorage shown here remembers table choice, not durable evidence. Speed Dating and general observations need additional recovery work. Do not present a universal “Everything is saved offline” banner.
- Avoid automatic navigation after a row save; the member may still be observing or typing. Do not refresh an entire active form in a way that drops selection, focus or uncertain writes. A changed conflict/permission must still revoke protected access immediately on the server.

Depends on canonical policy and RPC contracts, not a new control-room shell.
2. **P1: useful composition.** Operate/Schedule/Settings on event detail; Operate/Setup/Results on round detail; concise exception-to-action links, full slot times, honest attendance roll/coverage labels, member selected-person/team context and observation search. Reuse role/cycle routing. Deployable without a rotation engine.
3. **P1: roster/placement safety.** Canonical formation cohort/receipts; atomic reviewed transfer if desired; readback/changed-roster notices and no-loss navigation. Depends on group mutation restrictions, evidence preservation and authorized read models.
4. **P2: explicit linked plan.** Decide event↔round cardinality, room/rotation authority and published assignment model; then implement shared board current/next and personal instructions. Validate with operators before adding timers, synchronization or staffing optimization. Policy approval is required for exceptions, not for routine view rearrangement.

Cut from the initial scope: auto-scoring, AI evaluation summaries/ranking, social reactions on candidates, “most observed” leaderboards, another calendar product, automatic late penalties, full offline sync, global live-room map, drag/drop scheduling, new chat/notification channels and autonomous roster rebalancing. None is necessary to make the existing live workflows understandable.

## Scenario acceptance plan for the parent

These are proposed checks, not tests run in this planning task. Parent owns browser verification. Use synthetic difficult fixtures and actual role boundaries; do not expose real applicant data in recordings.

- **Door, phone, weak connection:** signed-in walk-up with no application, member with the same QR, duplicate self-check-in after lost response, connected staff fallback, and both devices offline. Confirm role-appropriate destinations and no false attendance receipt.
- **Late attendee:** within window, after closed window, no enrolled round, enrolled but unplaced, and group already under observation. Confirm no implicit enrollment, score change, retroactive evidence or policy bypass.
- **Speed Dating, busy phone:** two people with similar/long names, missing headshot, note before score, rapid taps, debounce then navigation, competing tabs and frozen round. Confirm intended versus saved data and exact conflict/retry behavior.
- **Group Case:** remembered team removed, applicant moved while an unsaved note exists, notes-only evidence, required criterion blank, 74% versus 75% weight, unknown quality, two records from one evaluator and genuine score spread. Confirm navigation never fabricates qualifying evidence.
- **Board scope:** operations-only, manager/panel, conflicted operator and wrong-cycle deep link. Confirm privileged results stay protected and unknown data stays unknown. Compare staffing and evidence on the same fixture.
- **Schedule/close:** DST boundary, overnight slots, occupied slots, cancelled event, stale/partial list, close with incomplete evidence, lost close acknowledgement and read-only return. Confirm no reopen/notify/release promise appears as a side effect.
- **Accessibility/layout:** 320/390px, keyboard, zoom/long content, focus return, soft keyboard, reduced motion and visible save/error announcements. The source-defined 44px controls are a design target, not a claim of verified conformance.

Review receipt: read the project instructions, product/design/policy rules, shared design workflow, current route components, relevant write endpoints/types and latest applicable check-in/grading/group migrations.
