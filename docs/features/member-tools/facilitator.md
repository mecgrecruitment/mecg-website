# AI sprint facilitator preparation

Keep the actual answer key and held-out questions in a restricted facilitator source, not in the member browser bundle. The six EX documents and three visible practice questions are synthetic teaching material, not company or MECG policy.

Before inviting teams, confirm a facilitator, separate reviewer, viable tool access, costs and a calendar window. Agree whether to adopt the proposed five-dimension 0–3 rubric and threshold (10/15, evaluation and handoff each at least 2). No target is treated as adopted by this build.

Create 12 held-out questions before submissions: six answerable from current documents, three deliberately unknown, three requiring rejection of the superseded guidance or careful reconciliation. For each, privately record expected facts, source IDs, allowable variations and error severity. Do not reuse the three public practice questions as held-out tests. Have another facilitator check that each expected answer is supported and the unknowns are truly missing. Freeze a version and do not optimize demos against that key.

Run the manual baseline and demo with the same held-out tasks. Record source citations, correctness, unsupported claims, abstentions, elapsed time including verification, setup time, tool/model/settings and reruns of ambiguous failures. A faster wrong answer is a failure. Separate observed effort from projected savings. Record rubric dimension scores and reasons in the independent review findings, linking the evaluation report.

Capture all starting teams and incomplete submissions in the authoritative restricted program register; this first submission workspace is not a registration/attendance system and cannot supply a valid program completion denominator by itself. Report aggregate participation, matched learning-check coverage, actual costs and facilitator effort. Collect sharing permission separately; do not turn participation into permission for public demos or sponsor reporting of personal data.
