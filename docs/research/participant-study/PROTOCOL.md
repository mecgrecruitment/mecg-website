# MECG designer and user acceptance study

Status: historical optional human-study preparation, no participants enrolled or evidence collected. The user subsequently clarified that agents should act as the requested panel; the active AI review is tracked separately and does not generate human participant data.
Owner: site/recruitment owner. This protocol does not authorize invitations or external messages.

## Requirement and honest evidence

The requested end state includes agreement from a panel of 100 excellent designers and users. AI reviews, synthetic personas, scores, tests and this protocol do not satisfy it. Recruit 100 distinct consenting people, record relevant role/experience with their permission, and document the selection criteria before sessions. “Best” has no objective global ranking; the owner must identify the actual experts and intended users rather than imply an endorsement. Do not count an AI agent, repeat session, silent participant or ambiguous response as a person agreeing.

Use a staging revision with synthetic applicant/client records and isolated delivery. Record the exact revision, viewport/browser, assistive technology where volunteered, and session date. Exclude real applicant data, resumes, access needs, outcomes and credentials. Keep signed consent, recordings and contact information outside the repository in an owner-controlled research store; use participant codes in analysis. Consent covers the tasks and storage, and withdrawal must be possible.

## Participants and tasks

Include prospective clients, students from several majors and years, current reviewers, recruitment operators, and experienced interface/accessibility designers. Record the cohort composition and expertise separately; no ratio is invented as an acceptance condition. Each participant does relevant tasks first and a holistic review afterward. Do not show evaluators AI praise, award references or other participants' judgments before their own responses.

1. **Comprehension:** after the first screen, explain what MECG offers, who it is for, and the next step for you. Record the participant's answer before prompting.
2. **Client fit:** find cost, engagement length, team, client responsibilities and confidentiality guidance. Distinguish factual answers from assumptions; locate a published case or recognize that case-study approval is pending.
3. **Inquiry:** send a synthetic project inquiry in the isolated environment; explain the confirmation and what happens next. Stop before any real email or account write.
4. **Student preparation:** identify eligibility, required materials, live dates versus unavailable dates, evaluation criteria, and the difference between open events and evaluated rounds.
5. **Application recovery:** write synthetic answers, interrupt saving, reload, compare conflicting copies and choose deliberately. Two tabs must not overwrite each other. Explain saved versus submitted state and verify the submission receipt.
6. **Reviewer work:** use synthetic behavior evidence, keyboard controls and N/O; identify missing criteria and evidence before final submission. Explain whether a saved observation is a valid evaluation and whether a completed round is ready.
7. **Operator work:** select a cohort across filters, review hidden selections, cancel a close action, inspect incomplete coverage, and release a synthetic mixed eligible/blocked batch. Identify every skipped applicant's safe reason and useful next step. No live round is closed.
8. **Accessibility:** traverse relevant tasks with keyboard, zoom, reduced motion and any volunteered assistive technology. Record actual obstacles and assistance; do not infer conformance from completion alone.

Use realistic tasks without telling participants where controls are. Record success, failure, assistance, wrong turns, misunderstanding and elapsed time separately. Ask what was confusing and what they expected before giving explanations. Treat operator/privacy errors as material even if a participant likes the visuals.

## Agreement and iteration

Ask a neutral explicit question after tasks: “Considering the design, clarity, accessibility and workflows you reviewed, do you consider this revision a world-class website for its purpose?” Record Agree / Disagree / Unsure, verbatim reason and unreviewed areas. Do not map a numerical rating, no complaint, or incomplete session to agreement. Also record whether they recommend release and any conditions; these are separate questions.

For every material finding, link the evidence, affected task, severity, implementation change and retest on the resulting revision. Never discard negative/unsure responses to create unanimity. If the user continues to require all 100 to agree, the goal remains unproven until 100 distinct actual participants explicitly agree to the same identified revision after relevant review, with unresolved material problems disclosed. Re-review materially changed areas; previous-revision votes are historical evidence, not current consensus.

## Handoff

The owner supplies the participant/research-service workflow and staging access. The agent can prepare fixtures, analyze de-identified evidence and implement observed fixes. Outbound recruiting, scheduling, data sharing and public publication require their actual authorization. Completion must cite the real research evidence and the matching revision; no panel has been claimed or simulated.
