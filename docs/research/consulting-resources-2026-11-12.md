# Consulting resource research

Owner: Codex research agent, supporting Rahil’s explicit website expansion request. Reviewed November 12, 2026. Status: source verification complete; website integration/deployment owned by parent.

All sixteen entries below use first-party sources. Public page contents were retrieved through web search/open. Accounts, downloads, product licenses, course completion and interactive tool flows were not exercised. No Drive changes, private records or external messages. Purpose statements explain useful applications; they are not claimed MECG policies or evidence of outcomes. No guaranteed certificate, job or partnership claims.

## Verified candidates

### 1. McKinsey interview preparation

- Source: [McKinsey interview preparation](https://www.mckinsey.com/careers/interviewing)
- Category: Case interview practice
- Use: Practice sample business cases and prepare detailed personal-experience examples.
- Access and limits: Public guidance and sample cases; no purchase needed to read. This does not grant access to assessments or imply MECG selection policy.

### 2. BCG case interview preparation

- Source: [BCG case interview preparation](https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/case-interview-preparation)
- Category: Case interview practice
- Use: Practice structuring a business challenge, explaining calculations and clarifying assumptions.
- Access and limits: Public guidance. Linked OneDay simulation has a separate sign-up; participation was not tested.

### 3. Bain case interview preparation

- Source: [Bain case interview preparation](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/case-interview/)
- Category: Case interview practice
- Use: Use Bain’s Coffee Shop Co. and FashionCo. practice cases and case-performance guidance.
- Access and limits: Public hub and linked sample cases. External prep products linked from it have separate terms; do not assume those products are entirely free.

### 4. IDEO.org interview method

- Source: [IDEO.org interview method](https://www.designkit.org/methods/2.html)
- Category: Client discovery and strategy
- Use: Prepare interview roles and broad-to-specific questions; distinguish observations from interpretation.
- Access and limits: Method text publicly readable. Site also offers accounts; no account created. Obtain appropriate participant consent and protect confidential notes.

### 5. Stanford Design Thinking Bootleg

- Source: [Stanford Design Thinking Bootleg](https://dschool.stanford.edu/tools/design-thinking-bootleg)
- Category: Client discovery and strategy
- Use: Choose activities for empathizing, defining, ideating, prototyping and testing.
- Access and limits: Public page with downloadable PDF. Explicit CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license: retain attribution and check noncommercial/share-alike restrictions before adapting or using in a paid engagement.

### 6. Strategyzer Business Model Canvas

- Source: [Strategyzer Business Model Canvas](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas)
- Category: Client discovery and strategy
- Use: Map customers, value, delivery, costs and revenue on one page, then identify assumptions to investigate.
- Access and limits: Official tool advertised as free to download and share with the team; guided playbooks are paid. Download flow and reuse terms should be checked before redistribution.

### 7. SBA market research and competitive analysis

- Source: [SBA market research and competitive analysis](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/)
- Category: Market research and data
- Use: Frame research around demand, market size, location, saturation, pricing and competitors.
- Access and limits: Public guidance with market-research and competitive-analysis sections. Old /business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis URL redirects here.

### 8. Census Business Builder

- Source: [Census Business Builder](https://www.census.gov/data/data-tools/cbb.html)
- Category: Market research and data
- Use: Find demographic and business data by location and business type; compare areas and download reports.
- Access and limits: Public Census page and linked tool. Check data vintage, geography and definitions before using figures; interactive download workflow not exercised.

### 9. BLS data tools

- Source: [BLS data tools](https://www.bls.gov/data/)
- Category: Market research and data
- Use: Find official labor, wage, employment, inflation and productivity series for market context.
- Access and limits: Public tables and data tools; API also offered. Check units, seasonal adjustment, geography and reference period before comparing series.

### 10. World Bank indicators

- Source: [World Bank indicators](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator)
- Category: Market research and data
- Use: Find country-level economic, population and sector indicators for international market context.
- Access and limits: Public indicator catalog. Coverage and methodology vary by indicator; check source notes, units, reference years and licensing for any selected dataset.

### 11. Microsoft Excel help and learning

- Source: [Microsoft Excel help and learning](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/)
- Category: Analysis skills
- Use: Use official spreadsheet help and learning material while preparing a workbook or analysis.
- Access and limits: Public help content. Excel application access is separate and may require a license; no student entitlement assumed. Older video-training URL redirects here.

### 12. Microsoft Learn for Power BI

- Source: [Microsoft Learn for Power BI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi)
- Category: Analysis skills
- Use: Learn data connection, Power Query preparation, modeling, reports and visualization through official learning paths.
- Access and limits: Public training catalog and learning pages. Sign-in may be used for progress; product sharing, instructor-led training and certification have separate access/cost requirements.

### 13. Atlassian project plan template

- Source: [Atlassian project plan template](https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence/templates/project-plan)
- Category: Project delivery
- Use: Define project roles, scope, milestones and reference materials before delivery starts.
- Access and limits: Public instructions and example. Using the hosted template requires an Atlassian workspace/account and its plan limits; no club subscription assumed.

### 14. Atlassian project poster

- Source: [Atlassian project poster](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/project-poster)
- Category: Project delivery
- Use: Keep the project problem, assumptions, supporting evidence and possible solutions in a living reference.
- Access and limits: Public play instructions; hosted templates/tools have separate account requirements. Can apply the planning questions in an approved existing workspace.

### 15. Atlassian DACI decision framework

- Source: [Atlassian DACI decision framework](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci)
- Category: Project delivery
- Use: Clarify the driver, approver, contributors and informed participants for a specific decision.
- Access and limits: Public instructions. Optional Confluence template requires its own account; this is a proposed method, not MECG approval authority.

### 16. Atlassian retrospective

- Source: [Atlassian retrospective](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/retrospective)
- Category: Project delivery
- Use: Review what worked, identify improvements and assign follow-through actions after a project milestone.
- Access and limits: Public play instructions. Optional Atlassian tools/templates have separate account terms; do not upload client information without authorization.
