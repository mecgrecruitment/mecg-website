# MECG identity exploration review

17 October 2026. Scope: responsive web palette and design essence study in `explore.html`, not a production redesign, full portal review, native app review, or usability study. Reviewer did not edit the judged HTML.

## Method

Two full visual review rounds used T3 `html_preview`. In each round, all three panels were rendered at 728px and 360px. A third focused round rechecked the dark and orange panels at 360px after a mobile selector correction. Hidden panels were exposed only in temporary HTML strings. All fourteen previews reported no horizontal overflow. The review considered actual pixels, identity, color and life, task hierarchy, geometry, specificity, and evidence framing.

## Round 1 findings and response

1. The recommended Open studio plus Momentum combination was described but not shown. **Addressed:** the recommended panel now shows a strong blue public hero with real MECG photography and orange action.
2. Momentum's unequal-height stage shapes resembled a chart without metrics. **Addressed:** equal-height labeled objects express stages without implying quantities.
3. Adjoining public hero and member workspace appeared to form one page. **Addressed:** explicit context labels and a thick separator identify separate applications of the same identity.

## Round 2 result

The three requested changes visibly landed at both widths. The recommended direction now carries recognizable blue/orange energy and human presence while the member example preserves a clear next task. The mockup labels and repository-photo caption keep illustrative work and community evidence appropriately framed.

Round 2 found one material regression: a misplaced mobile selector gave every mobile hero headline a blue rectangle. **Addressed and visually verified in round 3:** `.brand h3,.field h3{font-size:37px}` now preserves the intended pale-blue serif headline on the dark ground and navy headline directly on the orange ground. Both 360px previews fit without overflow.

The minor Momentum copy mismatch is also corrected: its note now describes orange fields and blue focal points, matching the displayed study. Optional peer reference screenshots are collapsed by default, preserving focus on MECG's directions; the round 3 screenshots show the collapsed control without layout disruption.

No unresolved material issue remains within this narrow study. The chosen palette and essence remain proposals. This review does not establish contrast compliance, keyboard completion, authenticated flow quality, motion behavior, expanded peer-reference layout quality, or broad user preference; those require separate implementation verification.
