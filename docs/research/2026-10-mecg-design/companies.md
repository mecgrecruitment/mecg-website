# Company design benchmarks for MECG

Observed 17 October 2026. These are selected references for specific design strengths, not an objective ranking of the world's best companies. Evidence combines official page content with direct desktop browser inspection at 1280 × 800. Sites change by region, campaign, and session; this is a snapshot, not a full accessibility or usability audit. Palette descriptions below are visual observations, not official brand specifications. Recommendations are our interpretation.

## Stripe — technical confidence with visible energy

**Observed:** The homepage combines a white ground, dark navy typography, vivid purple actions, a large orange/pink/violet flowing visual, and fine structural rules. Its primary proposition and start/contact actions precede product demonstrations. Further down, named customer stories sit beside relevant products and contextual metrics. The technical offering has expressive visual material rather than a uniformly gray treatment. [Official homepage](https://stripe.com/)

**Transfer to MECG:** Keep operational surfaces clear, but give the public introduction a memorable blue/orange visual gesture. Show a concrete project artifact beside the problem it answered. Put client scope, semester timing, and a contact action before a long capabilities catalogue.

**Do not transfer:** Stripe's scale claims, enterprise breadth, or endless logo density. MECG's named past organizations do not establish measured client outcomes or endorsements. Do not copy its proprietary gradient identity.

## Linear — precise hierarchy and the work itself

**Observed:** The homepage uses an almost-black ground, large white headline, restrained gray navigation, fine rules, and a detailed product scene. Yellow issue state, avatars, labels, and project content introduce small concentrated accents. The hero makes the product recognizable through actual workflow structure. The separate Method page organizes principles into direction and building practices. [Official homepage](https://linear.app/), [Linear Method](https://linear.app/method)

**Transfer to MECG:** Design private pages around current work: next review, due date, assignee, save state, and useful progress. Use compact rows and strong grouping. Let real people, project thumbnails, and purposeful status accents carry life.

**Do not transfer:** Treat its dark palette as a universal premium recipe, or reproduce a software-marketing hero throughout recruitment and community pages. Tiny muted labels and a huge product scene do not automatically make MECG's forms clearer.

## Apple — one clear subject per scene

**Observed:** The current homepage stages individual products with large imagery, short centered copy, generous space, and a consistent pair of filled/outlined blue actions. Neutral surroundings make the physical object, hands, screen content, and product color the expressive material. Product campaigns are visually separated rather than packed into many equal cards. [Official homepage](https://www.apple.com/)

**Transfer to MECG:** Give each key section one job and one focal asset. A photographed working session, a readable annotated deliverable, or a strong member portrait can be more convincing than more interface decoration. Repeat reliable action styles.

**Do not transfer:** Vague campaign slogans that assume existing global awareness. MECG still needs to explain what engineering consulting means, who can join, what clients contribute, and what a semester delivers. A small club cannot rely on immaculate product renders to supply all its identity.

## IDEO — human creativity made tangible

**Observed:** The homepage opens with a dark blue-gray ground, an expressive outlined-letter logo, a sharp lime contact treatment, and large media showing people, workspaces, materials, and colorful workshop artifacts. Featured work uses a client and a specific problem or change, rather than an undifferentiated service list. The site explains a human-centered process and interdisciplinary work. [Official homepage](https://www.ideo.com/), [Work](https://www.ideo.com/work)

**Transfer to MECG:** This is the strongest conceptual reference for connecting technical rigor with human collaboration. Show how students investigate, model, test, and explain a problem; pair community imagery with useful project evidence. A photographed work surface can supply warmth and specificity.

**Do not transfer:** Borrow IDEO's claims, decades of authority, lime identity, or abstract transformation language. MECG should describe the real scale and boundaries of its student projects.

Browser evidence: `/home/rahil/.t3/userdata/browser-artifacts/browser-screenshot-www-ideo-com-mvcff5t1-d0efd964.png`. Consent overlay obscured the lower opening scene; observations concern visible material and official content.

## Airbnb — friendly specificity around a task

**Observed:** The session exposed a white interface, coral brand mark, saturated pink search action, rounded search grouping, small dimensional category illustrations, and explicit Where/When/Who input labels. Most of the page remained neutral. Listing photography did not load in this observed session, so no conclusion about its present listing composition is made. [Official homepage](https://www.airbnb.com/)

**Transfer to MECG:** Build approachability through labels, considerate forms, real people, and small warm visual touches. On applications, make requirements, dates, and the next action obvious. Treat related inputs as one coherent task.

**Do not transfer:** Marketplace navigation, exaggerated pill rounding everywhere, or a task model that doesn't fit club operations. A distinctive search shell is not a reason to make every panel float.

## Synthesis for MECG

**Proposed essence: collaborative engineering, visibly in motion.** This is a recommendation, not a factual claim about user perception. Combine Linear's hierarchy, Stripe's controlled energy, Apple's focal discipline, IDEO's visible making, and Airbnb's explicit task language. The result should feel capable, curious, and welcoming.

- Preserve recognizable MECG navy/blue/orange continuity, then give each color a job: navy for authority and reading, blue for primary interactive states, orange for concentrated brand energy, warm paper for breathing room. Choose exact tokens through contrast testing rather than sampling another company's page.
- Make real people and actual work the main sources of richness. Public scenes can carry larger color fields and photography; private scenes should concentrate expression in the active task, human presence, project identity, and progress.
- Build a single signature motif around investigation becoming a clear recommendation: an annotated question, evidence mark, or connecting line. Use it sparingly and consistently.
- Define geometry by role, not an undifferentiated rounded look: compact controls, modest panel corners, circular portraits, broad uninterrupted section grounds.
- Motion should confirm cause and effect: selection, save, progress, and expanding context. This research did not perform a motion/reduced-motion audit of these sites; do not claim their animations as verified best practice.
- Measure parity through clarity, coherence, specificity, accessibility, responsive behavior, and verified proof. Avoid a prestige scoreboard based on visual taste.

## Verification limits

Desktop opening scenes and official page content were inspected. This research did not test mobile layouts, keyboard completion, all menus, reduced motion, authenticated company applications, conversion, or user preference. No borrowed imagery is licensed for MECG production use by this research. Screenshot references document observations; they are not proposed MECG assets.
