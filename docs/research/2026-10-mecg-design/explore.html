MECG is a motion study: ideas take shape as teams move a question forward.

Boldest campaign direction: orange fields, oversized type and blue focal points. Useful for recruitment and event graphics; use smaller doses in everyday work.

Same content in each direction. Visual studies with illustrative private tasks; controls inside the mockups are nonfunctional.
Ink #12213EBlue #254F9FOrange #FE904DGround #F5F8FD

Colour with a job

Blue identifies MECG and primary workspace actions. Orange marks the next task and public calls to action. Dark orange #A83E0B handles orange text on light surfaces. Errors and success keep their own labeled semantic states.

Life from real material

People, working sessions and approved deliverable details. Natural colour, clear captions, no stock corporate handshakes. Existing culture photos show community; they do not prove client outcomes.

A consistent physical system

Spacing: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48. Controls: 8px corners; panels: 12px. 44px touch targets. Type: readable sans for work; expressive scale for public headlines.

One memorable behaviour

A saved task stays in place and changes to “Saved”; completing it reveals the next task. No surprise reordering while typing. Short state transitions, with reduced-motion support.

What MECG should learn from the benchmarks

View peer reference snapshots · MECC and AKPsi

Desktop opening scenes observed October 2026; references for comparison, not MECG assets.

MECC
Saturated blue and yellow, real-member collage. Bring comparable confidence to MECG’s own blue/orange, with fewer competing elements.
APEX / BOND
APEX connects services to detailed evidence; BOND makes people visible through documentary media. MECG needs both work credibility and belonging.
AKPsi
One immersive blue/gold material scene gives the identity presence. Borrow the confidence, then ground MECG’s richness in people and its own work.
DSP / PCT
Real chapter photography makes belonging tangible. Keep that warmth, with cleaner text placement and shorter routes to eligibility and preparation.
IDEO / Stripe
Visible making and controlled colour energy. Build a recognizable engineering studio identity with one strong visual subject per section.
Linear / Apple
Clear hierarchy and focal discipline. For private pages, the current task leads; brand expression supports it.

Current-site observations, October 2026. These are design references, not a ranking of club quality or prestige. The recommended palette is a proposal; the shipped design has not been changed.
