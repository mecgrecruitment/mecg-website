# Michigan consulting-club design benchmarks

Research date: 17 October 2026. Five official club homepages visually inspected in the T3 browser at 1280 × 800; supporting client pages read as primary sources. This is a design comparison, not a ranking of organizational quality, selectivity, outcomes, or prestige. Public pages do not reveal private member software. Descriptive judgments below are the researcher's interpretation of observed interfaces.

## Evidence and limits

- Club claims are self-published, not independently audited. Do not treat logos, testimonials or counts as verified measures of comparative excellence, or reuse them as MECG evidence.
- MECC and BOND both make historical leadership/oldest-type claims; this audit does not resolve or repeat those as facts. Historical university-hosted MECC subpages remain indexed and are not the same as its current homepage.
- Counts and historical details vary within some sites. MECG should keep each published fact in one maintained source rather than duplicate counts across prose and cards.
- 180DC's [homepage](https://www.180dcmichigan.com/) says all years can apply, while its [application FAQ](https://www.180dcmichigan.com/apply) mentions freshmen/sophomores. This is an observed content inconsistency, not a conclusion about its current admission policy. MECG can distinguish itself through unambiguous eligibility and live cycle dates.
- Typography names above were read from computed browser styles, not inferred from screenshots. Colour descriptions are visual observations, not official palette specifications.

## Recommended transferable principles

These are design recommendations inferred from the sample, not claims that the clubs themselves prescribe these rules.

1. **Actual people supply life.** Four sampled heroes use documentary people media; MECC combines people with a rich colour field. MECG should show work sessions, client presentations and informal community, using approved real photography.
2. **Evidence supplies authority.** Place the client question, method, tangible deliverable, semester and approved outcome together. A logo wall can support this but cannot replace it. APEX's detailed services page and MECC/Nexecon cases are useful models for structure.
3. **Two public intentions must be obvious.** A client should find work/services and engagement expectations; a student should find membership, eligibility and current recruitment. This can fit in a short navigation without an intimidating taxonomy.
4. **A recognisable colour has to occupy meaningful space.** The strongest differences in this sample come from actual surfaces, imagery and composition, not swapping one button hex. MECG's blue/orange can have an ownable presence through a deliberate colour field, editorial project image and consistent controls.
5. **MECG has room for an engineering-studio character.** Pair warm real-member imagery with intelligible evidence objects: an annotated map, a research extract, a model or a deliverable. This is a proposed differentiation from the sampled homepages, not a claim that no other club uses artifacts elsewhere.
6. **Bring identity into private tools with lower intensity.** Keep the same type, geometry, blue/orange roles and human imagery, but organise board/member pages around the next task. Public photographic heroes are not templates for application review screens.
