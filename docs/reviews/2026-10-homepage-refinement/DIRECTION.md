# Homepage refinement — October 17, 2026

## Brief and scope
User requested App Designer Pro refinement of the homepage only. This is an established responsive website. Preserve the Precision Instrument identity in docs/DESIGN.md, existing fonts, palette, routes, recruitment-driven student action and approved-project publication behavior. Native device chrome, paywalls, new visual identities, application/backend changes and deployment are outside scope.

## Concept and voice
MECG is an engineering document that helps organizations understand a consulting engagement. Use concrete descriptions, descriptive action verbs and factual engagement details. No invented results, approved case studies or membership schedule claims.

## Keep / refine
Keep both client and student routes; all four process stations and their readouts; mobile process disclosure; reduced motion; statistics; past organization labels; approval status; services; the captioned culture photograph; SEO metadata and semantic headings.
Refine the hero density, headline specificity, mobile line breaks, illustration framing, engagement facts, service typography and closing heading.

## Decisions
Keep the established navy, paper, blue and orange roles. Keep sans prose and mono figure labels. One orange primary action; the secondary student action remains driven by actual recruitment state. No additional runtime or image asset dependency.

Headline: “Clear decisions. Built on research.”
Intro: “We help organizations turn open questions into research, analysis, and practical recommendations. Led by University of Michigan students. No consulting fee.”
Facts: one academic semester; 6–8 Michigan students, from existing shared content.
Signature: existing four-station engagement bench; enlarge its camera framing without changing the mechanism, transitions or content.

## Review method
Two rendered rounds with one independent design critic. Use the shared T3 browser for current UI inspection. Adapt the app rubric to responsive web; do not imply Apple affiliation or real-user research. Screenshots do not prove usability. Preserve the existing identity rather than creating three replacement concepts for a scoped refinement.
